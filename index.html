Improved UAV Multi-trip Geohazard Planner

Plans multi-trip UAV monitoring routes for geohazard sites and compares four algorithms (TS4GM, LNS, GRASP, GA) on the same data, mirroring the comparative study in Wang and Wen (2026), Section 5.2. Everything runs inside your browser.

How to use this page

  1. Pick algorithms. TS4GM is always included as the reference; click LNS, GRASP or GA to add them to the comparison.
  2. Choose a setting. Quick is the only browser preset — Standard and Paper-style are far too slow for a tab and are available from the command line.
  3. Press Run. Results appear in the tabs on the right: Compare, Trips, Route map, Convergence, Downloads.
